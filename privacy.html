Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026.

recipes4humans rewrites recipes into a cooking view. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page says what it stores and how to remove it.

If you do not sign in

Everything stays in your browser. Recipes you paste are rewritten on your device and saved there. Nothing about you or your recipes is sent to us.

If you import a recipe from a link, our server fetches that page for you, because browsers cannot read other sites directly. The server does not keep the page or log which pages were fetched.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets your recipes follow you between your phone and your computer.

  • From Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google's account id. We use them only to know which recipes are yours and to show who is signed in. We do not ask for access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Your recipes are stored on our server so they can sync: the recipe text, its title, its source link, which lines you have ticked, and which recipes you have starred.
  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 90 days. It is the only cookie the site sets, and it is never used for tracking.

We never sell, share or publish your information or your recipes, and never use them for advertising or to train AI models.

Where it is stored

The site and its database run on Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes requests to the site as our hosting provider.

Deleting your information

  • Sign out (Library, Account) removes your recipes from that device. They stay in your account.
  • Delete my account (Library, Account) permanently removes your account, your sign-in sessions and every recipe stored on our server. Recipes on the device you are using stay there.
  • You can also remove recipes4humans from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

recipes4humans is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
